# EasyMoveZone Core Features

Final architecture for the three core product systems that define the platform.

## 1. Travel Intelligence Core

Visa + Laws + Decision Engine merged into one decision layer.

### Purpose

This is the intelligence engine that helps a traveler understand whether they can travel, whether they should travel, and what preparation is required.

### Capabilities

- Visa intelligence
  - Visa difficulty scoring
  - Required documents
  - Approval likelihood based on nationality
  - Processing timelines
  - Embassy locations and appointment availability
  - Alternative visa-friendly destinations
  - Common rejection reasons
  - Step-by-step guidance
- Legal and compliance navigator
  - Driving laws
  - Medication restrictions
  - Drone rules
  - Alcohol and tobacco laws
  - Police interaction norms
  - Photography restrictions
  - LGBTQ+ safety laws
  - Dress codes
  - Import and export rules
- Decision intelligence coach
  - Budget analysis
  - Risk tolerance scoring
  - Travel personality matching
  - Visa strength evaluation
  - Cultural comfort assessment
  - Best travel dates
  - Best routes
  - Best neighborhoods
  - Best transport modes

### User outcome

The traveler gets one unified decision report answering:

- Can I travel there?
- Should I travel there?
- What do I need to prepare?
- What risks or laws must I understand?
- What alternatives fit me better?

### Product role

This is the signature feature of EasyMoveZone.

## 2. Situational Awareness Core

Smart Map + Reality Check + Live News Events.

### Purpose

This is the traveler's real-time destination awareness layer.

### Capabilities

- Smart map safety layer
  - Safe zones
  - Legal zones for drones, alcohol, and smoking
  - Scam hotspots
  - Cultural zones
  - Neighborhood personality
  - Embassy locations
  - Emergency services
- Country reality check
  - Honest safety assessment
  - Police strictness
  - Cost levels
  - Scam frequency
  - Cultural sensitivity
  - Night safety for solo travelers
- Live news event map
  - Protests
  - Weather alerts
  - Political unrest
  - Transport strikes
  - Natural disasters
  - Major crime incidents
  - Health outbreaks

### User outcome

The traveler sees:

- What's happening right now
- Where it's safe
- Where laws are strict
- Where scams are common
- Where cultural norms differ
- Where embassies and help are located

### Product role

This becomes the Google Maps of travel safety and live destination awareness.

## 3. Travel Execution Core

Readiness Checklist + Persona Engine + Booking Integrations.

### Purpose

This is the operational layer that gets the traveler ready and connects them to action.

### Capabilities

- Travel readiness checklist
  - Visa documents
  - Vaccines
  - Insurance
  - Local apps
  - Offline maps
  - Cultural prep
  - Legal prep
  - Emergency numbers
- Travel persona engine
  - Explorer
  - Relaxer
  - Culture seeker
  - Food traveler
  - Nightlife traveler
  - Budget optimizer
  - Luxury traveler
- Booking integrations
  - Visa application services
  - Accommodation platforms
  - Transportation platforms including flights, trains, and buses

### User outcome

The traveler gets:

- A personalized preparation plan
- A persona-based travel strategy
- Direct links to book visas, accommodation, and transport

### Product role

This turns EasyMoveZone into a travel preparation and execution hub.

## Summary

- Core Feature 1: Travel Intelligence Core
  - Visa + Laws + Decision Engine
- Core Feature 2: Situational Awareness Core
  - Smart Map + Reality Check + Live News Events
- Core Feature 3: Travel Execution Core
  - Readiness Checklist + Persona Engine + Booking Integrations
